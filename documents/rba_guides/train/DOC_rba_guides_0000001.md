# Risk based approach teaching guide

SYNTHETIC TRAINING MATERIAL. Fictional data for Project SENTINEL exercises. Not an official circular, legal guidance, human-reviewed evidence or production model output.

Risk assessment combines amount, account tenure, observed device and city changes, failed outcomes and velocity. Inherent contextual risk is distinct from residual risk after a documented verification control. Record likelihood, impact, evidence confidence, mitigating controls and the reason for escalation. This data factory does not conduct KYC, AML screening or a legal RBA assessment. Use the known fraud targets only after prediction when assessing model quality. The fraud score simulator is uncalibrated and cannot establish production cutoffs.
