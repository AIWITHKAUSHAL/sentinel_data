# Training splits and label availability guide

SYNTHETIC TRAINING MATERIAL. Fictional data for Project SENTINEL exercises. Not an official circular, legal guidance, human-reviewed evidence or production model output.

Authorisations from January through October 2025 form the retrospective training split. November and December 2025 form validation. January and February 2026 are holdout. All labels are mature at the May 2026 snapshot. Retrospective splitting does not recreate a model deployed on November first: use eligible_at_next_split_start for that stricter backtest. CPT candidates also require a publication date before November first. MCC encoding uses earlier training labels already mature at the frozen month boundary.
