# Investigator evidence and case writing guide

SYNTHETIC TRAINING MATERIAL. Fictional data for Project SENTINEL exercises. Not an official circular, legal guidance, human-reviewed evidence or production model output.

Write the authorisation facts first, unknowns second, and later findings in a separate dated section. Preserve the source transaction and document identifier. A decline is not a fraud label. A dispute is not necessarily an unauthorised payment. The synthetic verdict is known sixty days after the transaction. Human review is required to turn generated candidates into gold investigation examples.
